How It Works

KNOW → WAIT → TRACK → TRAVEL

The complete electric bus journey experience in four simple steps. From knowing your route to arriving at your destination.

01

KNOW

Find your nearest stop, check available routes, and understand which bus serves your corridor.

02

WAIT

Signal to the driver you are waiting. Your aggregated count reaches the driver's in-cab tablet instantly.

03

TRACK

Watch your bus move in real-time on the live map. Know when it leaves each stop and exactly when it arrives.

04

TRAVEL

Board your bus, scan your QR code, and enjoy a clean zero-emission journey across Kampala and beyond.

Transforming Kampala Transit

Designed for Commuters & Fleet Operators

Bridging the information gap between waiting passengers on the street and electric transit dispatchers.

PAX

For Kampala Commuters

Convenience & Certainty

Eliminates unpredictable stage waiting times and gives passengers total visibility over their daily commute.

  • ✓ Never Miss a Bus: Live GPS simulation and stop countdowns tell you exactly when to leave home or office.
  • ✓ Signal Approaching Drivers: The "I'm Waiting" beacon prevents buses from driving past full or empty stages.
  • ✓ Cashless QR Ticketing: Seamless simulated Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel) ticketing with instant digital boarding passes.
  • ✓ Clean, Quiet Travel: Track zero tailpipe emission buses that reduce Kampala's urban air pollution.
  • ✓ Universal Accessibility: Works on any smartphone browser, low-bandwidth networks, and screen readers.
OPS

For Transport Operators & KCCA

Efficiency & Data-Driven Control

Provides centralized telematics and real-time passenger crowd analytics to optimize bus scheduling.

  • ✓ Demand Heatmap: Real-time visualization of queue sizes at all 18 stages to prevent bottlenecks.
  • ✓ Dynamic Relief Dispatch: Deploy standby electric coaches dynamically to overwhelmed corridors.
  • ✓ Battery & SoC Telemetry: Monitor electric battery percentage and schedule charging at Bweyogerere hub.
  • ✓ Distraction-Free Driver Tablet: High-contrast in-cab interface showing waiting commuters ahead.
  • ✓ Open Transport Data (GTFS): Structured data ready to plug into municipal transit databases and GPS trackers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the "WAIT" feature help me get a bus?

When you signal that you are waiting at a specific stop, the approaching driver's in-cab tablet immediately shows how many commuters are waiting. This ensures drivers do not bypass your stage.

Is the ETA shown on the app accurate?

The ETA is a prototype estimate calculated using bus coordinates, stop distance, and estimated speed. Future versions will include real GPS data, traffic, and road conditions.

What is the fare for an electric bus journey?

Demo fares range from UGX 2,500 to UGX 5,000 depending on the route. These are prototype values only.

About This Platform

About E-Bus Connect Uganda

E-Bus Connect Uganda is an academic software engineering prototype designed to demonstrate how a modern electric public transport platform could serve Kampala and Uganda's growing metropolitan areas.

Inspired by Uganda's Kiira Motors Kayoola EVS electric buses and the Kampala Metropolitan transport system, this prototype showcases the KNOW → WAIT → TRACK → TRAVEL passenger journey paradigm.

Important Disclaimer

This is a student software engineering prototype. It is not officially connected to the Ugandan government, KCCA, Kiira Motors, or any real eBus system. All data is simulated. No real payments are processed.
